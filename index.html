BEHIND THE SCENES

What Nobody Else Sees

01

The Nerves Before

Every race starts the same way for him — quiet, focused, a little scared underneath. He never says it out loud. I just know, because his jokes get worse right before the gun goes off.

02

The Calm After

Something happens to his shoulders the second he crosses a line. All that held breath just — leaves. That's my favorite version of him. Tired, proud, finally still.

03

Steadying My Chaos

I show up late, overthink everything, forget the plan halfway through. He just laughs, resets it, and starts again with me. Never once made me feel like too much.

04

Still Laughing

Somewhere between arguing about turning 26 versus 30 and planning a life we haven't started yet, we're just two people who keep choosing to laugh about it. That's the whole story, really.

A VERSE, FROM ME

Written by Vandana

I write in storms — half-finished, loud, undone,

you run through mine like it's just another run.

I burn the toast, I burn the plan, the day,

you lace your shoes and love me anyway.

Somewhere a stopwatch is counting your stride —

I'm not timing you, love. I'm just along for the ride.

Every race you finish, some new distance I see:

the calm you keep chasing looks a lot like me, and you, and we.

EPISODE THREE

Us, in Short

She creates the chaos.

He handles it. Gently. Every single time.

"Fuel for me, poison for you" — his idea of a warning label. A whole argument about turning 26 versus 30, settled the way we settle everything: half joking, fully sure. That's us — a little dramatic, mostly ridiculous, entirely ours.
